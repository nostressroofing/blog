Roofing advice that actually helps

Plain-language roofing advice for Saskatoon homeowners: what goes wrong on prairie roofs, what it costs to ignore, and what you can do about it before it gets expensive.

  • How to Prevent Ice Dams in a Prairie Winter

    Snow melts on the upper roof and refreezes at the cold eaves, and the ice ridge forces water back under your shingles. Heating cables along the eaves and gutters keep water draining — that is the fix that works.

  • When to Clean Your Gutters in Saskatoon (and Why Twice Matters)

    Clean once in late May after the seeds and blossoms drop, and again in October after the leaves fall. Two cleanings is what keeps water away from your foundation.

  • How Long Do Shingles Really Last in Saskatchewan?

    Shingles rated for 25 to 30 years only last 50 to 75 percent of that here — expect 15 to 18 years before they start failing. Curling edges, bare patches, and cracking shingles mean they are aging out.

  • What a Roof Inspection Actually Checks

    Shingles show age and wear, flashing reveals where leaks start, eavestroughs tell you if water is draining properly, and attic ventilation exposes trapped moisture cooking your roof from the inside.

  • Getting Your Roof Ready for Winter

    Before the snow flies: clear the gutters, trim branches hanging over the roof, check flashing for gaps, and make sure attic vents are open so warm air can escape.

  • Repair or Replace? How to Tell What Your Roof Needs

    Patch it when damage is isolated to a few shingles. Replace when you are seeing widespread granule loss, curling across large areas, or leaks in more than one spot.
