What to ask any roofer

Whether you call the company above or shop around, these questions separate the good from the sketchy:

  • Are your crews in-house or subcontracted?
  • What workmanship warranty do you offer, in writing?
  • Can I see photos of recent local jobs?
  • Will I get a written quote before work starts?
